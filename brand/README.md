# Pino's look

Pino is a drop that became a character: one soft tip, a round full body, big eyes, cheeks and a very
expressive mouth, little arms and legs. The pine is almost subliminal: the bright green. It has to
work tiny too: app icon, favicon, sticker.

- `pino-sheet.png`: the official character, eight poses (generated with OpenAI from Alessio's
  direction). `tools/cut_stickers.py` cuts it into the transparent pictures in `site/img/`.
- `pino-first-try-sheet.png`: the first try, a little tree with a face. Kept for the story.
- `pino.svg`: the simple face, drawn by hand, for what has to be tiny: the app icon (Android:
  `android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher.xml`, and `ic_pino.xml` for the white silhouette in
  notifications) and the site's favicon.

Colours: body `#3cc23e`, light `#8ee68a`, outline `#0e3b14`, cheeks `#ff9a8a`, mouth `#e5484d`.
