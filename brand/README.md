# Pino's look

Pino is a drop that became a character: one soft tip, a round full body, big eyes, cheeks and a very
expressive mouth, little arms and legs. The pine is almost subliminal: the bright green and, at most,
a tiny fork at the tip. It has to work tiny too: app icon, favicon, sticker.

- `pino.svg`: the simple face, drawn by hand. It is the app icon (Android:
  `android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher.xml`, and `ic_pino.xml` for the white silhouette in
  notifications), the site's favicon and hero (`site/pino.svg`).
- The illustrated character (poses and expressions) is generated with an image model (OpenAI or
  Gemini), starting from the sheets made so far. The chosen sheets go in this folder as PNG.

Colours: body `#3cc23e`, light `#8ee68a`, outline `#0e3b14`, cheeks `#ff9a8a`, mouth `#e5484d`.
